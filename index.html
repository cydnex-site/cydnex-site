How we work with you

A partner, not just a report.

We sit with your team, explain every finding in plain words and stay until the fixes are verified.

Approach

Four steps from scope to a safer system

The same method for every engagement, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Understand and discover

    Agree scope and rules of engagement, review the architecture and map the attack surface.

  2. 2

    Assess, validate and simulate

    Automated tools plus manual testing. Every finding is validated, with controlled exploitation to show real impact.

  3. 3

    Analyse and report

    CVSS-scored findings, an executive summary, technical evidence with reproduction steps and prioritised fixes.

  4. 4

    Remediate and improve

    We help prioritise fixes and retest them, so the work ends with confirmed results.

Broken access control on the invoice API

OWASP A01:2021CWE 639Asset /api/invoices/{id}

Evidence

GET /api/invoices/1043     (signed in as user A)
200 OK  {"customer":"user B","total":"48,200"}

Fix

  1. Check on the server that the invoice belongs to the signed-in account.
  2. Add an automated test for cross-account access.
  3. Use unguessable identifiers as a second layer.

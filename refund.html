Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

We want refunds to be simple and fair. All amounts here include GST, and there are no cancellation or processing fees.

On this page
  1. Courses (online and classroom)
  2. Security and IT services
  3. How to ask for a refund

Courses (online and classroom)

When you cancelWhat you get back
Before the batch startsFull refund
Within the first 7 days of the batchFull refund
After 7 daysNo refund, but you can move once to a later batch for free
We cancel or reschedule a batchFull refund, or a free move to another batch, your choice

Security and IT services

Refunds for services follow the signed contract. In general, any advance for work not yet started is refunded in full if either side cancels before work begins.

How to ask for a refund

Email academy@cydnex.com with your name, course or invoice number and the reason. We confirm within 3 working days and send the money back to the original payment method within 7 working days after that.

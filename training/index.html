Questions

Before you join

Who can join?

Students, graduates and working professionals. The Foundation track needs no experience. Advanced tracks list what you should know first.

Do I need my own laptop?

Yes, a laptop with at least 8 GB of RAM. We help you set up the lab software in the first session.

I am under 18. Can I join?

Yes, with your parent or guardian's written consent before the course starts, as Indian data protection law requires.

What do the fees include?

Live sessions, lab access, course materials, the project review and the certificate. Fees are shared before you join and always include GST, with no hidden charges.

Is the certificate a degree?

No. It is a Cydnex certificate of completion that shows your practical skills. It is not a university or government qualification.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. See our Refund Policy: a full refund before the batch starts or within the first 7 days.

Do you guarantee a job?

No one honestly can. We help with interview practice, CV reviews and referrals where we can.

Do you also work with companies?

Yes. For security testing, monitoring or staff awareness training for your company, visit cydnex.com.

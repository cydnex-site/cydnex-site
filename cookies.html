Third-party services

The only optional third-party service is Google Fonts, which loads our typefaces. When it loads, Google receives your IP address. It loads only if you click "Allow Google Fonts". If you choose "Use device fonts", the site uses fonts already on your device and contacts no third party.

Clicking WhatsApp or email on our contact form opens those apps, which have their own privacy policies.

Change your choice

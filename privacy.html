Who we are

Cydnex Technologies Private Limited ("Cydnex", "we", "us") runs this website and provides cybersecurity services and training (Cydnex Academy) from Hyderabad, India.

For any privacy question, contact our Grievance Officer: Dwarakanathan Yajamanyam, security@cydnex.com, Madhapur, Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to reply to you and to deliver what you ask for.

The website itself does not store what you type into the enquiry form. It opens your own WhatsApp or email app, and the message reaches us only when you press send.

Why we are allowed to use it

We use your data with your consent (for example, when you tick the box on our form) or for "legitimate uses" allowed by law, such as performing a contract you asked for or keeping tax records. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing does not affect anything we did before.

Children and students under 18

Some of our students may be under 18. Under the DPDP Act, we need verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian before we process the personal data of anyone under 18.

  • Our forms ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older, or that your parent or guardian agrees.
  • For course enrolment of anyone under 18, we ask the parent or guardian to confirm consent in writing (email or signed form) before the course starts.
  • We do not track, profile or send targeted advertising to children.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as our email provider, WhatsApp (Meta), our hosting provider (Hostinger) and our accountant, each only for that purpose.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. We use only providers with appropriate safeguards and follow any restrictions the Government of India sets on transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Students and clients: for the course or contract, then as long as tax and company law require (usually 8 years for invoices).
  • Security test data from client systems: handled under each client contract and deleted or returned at the end of the engagement.

How we protect it

We are a security company, so we apply the same care we recommend to clients: access only for staff who need it, multi-factor sign-in on our accounts, encrypted storage and devices, and a process to report any personal data breach to the Data Protection Board of India and to affected people as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and who we shared it with;
  • correct or complete it;
  • delete it (see below);
  • withdraw consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to;
  • for UK residents: object to processing or receive a copy of your data.

We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Delete my data

Want us to delete your data? Email us from the address you used with us, and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices, and we will tell you what that is.

Emails and messages from us

We send marketing emails or WhatsApp messages only if you agree. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time. Messages about a course or service you bought are not marketing.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell students and clients directly.

Responsible disclosure

Found a security issue? Tell us.

We welcome reports from security researchers and treat them seriously, as we expect our clients to.

How to report

Email security@cydnex.com with the affected page or system, the steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has. Our contact details are also in our security.txt file.

What we promise

  • We acknowledge your report within 3 working days.
  • We keep you updated until it is fixed.
  • We will not take legal action against research done in good faith that follows these rules.
  • With your permission, we thank you by name on this page once the issue is fixed.

In scope

  • cydnex.com and its subdomains

Please do not

  • Run denial-of-service or high-volume automated scans
  • Access, change or delete data beyond the minimum needed to show the issue
  • Use phishing, social engineering or physical attacks
  • Share the issue publicly before we have fixed it

Usually out of scope

  • Missing security headers or best-practice settings with no real impact
  • Reports from automated tools without a working proof
  • Issues in third-party services we do not control

We do not run a paid bug bounty yet. Client systems are never in scope here: those are tested only under signed contracts.

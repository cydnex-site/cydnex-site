Industries

Scoped to the way your sector works.

Different sectors face different rules and different attackers. We plan every engagement around yours.

Sector

BFSI and fintech

Banks, NBFCs and fintechs face RBI IT guidelines, payment fraud and constant audit pressure. We run VAPT and control reviews mapped to regulatory expectations, without slowing product releases.

We help you prepare for: RBI IT framework, PCI DSS, ISO 27001

Sector

Healthcare

Hospitals and health-tech platforms cannot afford downtime: ransomware and patient-data exposure put patients and licences at risk. We secure clinical systems, patient portals and device networks.

We help you prepare for: DPDP Act, sector norms, HIPAA for US clients

Sector

Government and public sector

Citizen portals and agency networks are high-value targets. We deliver authorised assessments mapped to CERT-In directions and ISO 27001, with clear evidence for audits and leadership reviews. Where a project requires a CERT-In empanelled auditor, we tell you upfront.

We help you prepare for: CERT-In directions, ISO 27001

Sector

E-commerce and retail

Festival seasons amplify fraud. We protect checkout, wallets, loyalty programmes and admin back offices from carding, credential stuffing and business-logic abuse.

We help you prepare for: PCI DSS, DPDP Act

Sector

Telecommunications

Telecom stacks hold massive subscriber data and connect every other industry. We assess self-care apps, OSS/BSS edges and signalling-adjacent exposure.

We help you prepare for: DoT and TRAI security expectations, ISO 27001

Sector

Manufacturing

IT and factory systems (OT) are converging on Indian shop floors. We assess ICS/SCADA boundaries, protect industrial IP and keep production lines running while hardening connected plants.

We help you prepare for: IEC 62443, ISO 27001

Sector

Education and edtech

Campuses and learning platforms hold student data and research IP on open networks. We secure portals, payment flows and labs without breaking the openness education needs.

We help you prepare for: DPDP Act (including children's data)

Sector

Energy and utilities

Grid and utility operators face critical-infrastructure threats. We deliver OT security reviews, control-centre IT checks and resilience testing aligned to Indian power-sector guidance.

We help you prepare for: CEA power-sector cyber guidelines, IEC 62443

Sector

Automotive and mobility

Connected vehicles, dealer systems and supplier networks link cars to the cloud. We test vehicle apps, dealer and service portals, and supplier connections, from India to the UK.

We help you prepare for: ISO/SAE 21434 and UNECE R155 readiness, ISO 27001

Sector

Pharma and life sciences

Research IP, clinical-trial data and regulated production lines make pharma a prime target, and Hyderabad one of its biggest hubs. We secure labs, data and plant systems without slowing validated processes.

We help you prepare for: DPDP Act, 21 CFR Part 11 data integrity, IEC 62443

Sector

SaaS and technology

Your product is your security reputation. We secure multi-tenant platforms, APIs and CI/CD pipelines, and produce SOC 2 and ISO 27001 evidence for enterprise sales in India and overseas.

We help you prepare for: SOC 2, ISO 27001
